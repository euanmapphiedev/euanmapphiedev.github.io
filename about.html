My journey into GIS began with a fascination for how place shapes everything — how geography underpins decisions in public health, urban planning, environmental management, and beyond.

Through my studies and independent projects, I've developed expertise across the full geospatial stack: from data collection and spatial analysis in ArcGIS Pro and QGIS, to building interactive web maps using JavaScript and Leaflet.js. I'm particularly drawn to the intersection of data science and cartography — using Python scripting and spatial statistics to surface patterns that aren't visible in raw data alone.

Outside of GIS, I'm interested in how maps communicate — how visual choices shape what people understand and believe about the world. I aim to make work that's technically rigorous and visually honest.

Currently seeking roles in geospatial development, GIS analysis, or web mapping where I can keep building at the edge of what maps can do.
